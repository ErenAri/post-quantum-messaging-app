PQMsg Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-04-08

Developer and contact
- This Android client is part of the PQMsg pilot project.
- Privacy inquiries and deletion requests can be initiated through the project issue tracker:
  https://github.com/ErenAri/post-quantum-messaging-app/issues

What this app is
- PQMsg is a pilot secure-messaging client for direct messaging and opaque private groups.
- Web messaging and calling are outside the supported Android pilot scope.

Data processed by the app and relay
- Account identifiers such as @username, device ID, and cryptographic public keys.
- Optional profile fields such as display name, shareable username, and avatar bytes.
- Push token registration data when notifications are configured.
- Encrypted message ciphertext, encrypted attachment blobs, delivery timestamps, and device-routing metadata needed to deliver messages.
- Contact and invite metadata that you explicitly create, including exact @username lookups and manual invite links.
- Local encrypted state on this phone such as keys, sessions, conversation state, contact pins, private-group state, and cached message history.
- Operational and security records such as audit events, delivery counters, rate-limit events, and integrity logs.

What the service does not require by default
- Phone-number registration is not required for the supported pilot path.
- Message content is intended to remain end-to-end encrypted; the relay is designed to process routing and encrypted payload storage rather than plaintext conversation content.

How data is used
- To provision your account and devices.
- To deliver direct messages, private-group messages, and attachments.
- To verify identity changes, trust pins, and transparency state.
- To operate the pilot environment, investigate abuse, and keep the service available.

How data is shared
- Data may be processed by the pilot operator and the infrastructure providers used to host the relay, database, backups, metrics, and logs.
- We do not describe the relay as a general-purpose advertising or analytics platform.

Retention and deletion
- Local encrypted state remains on this phone until you reset local state or delete the account.
- Deleting the account from the app is intended to remove the account record and associated user data from the relay.
- Limited records may remain where necessary for security, abuse prevention, fraud handling, backup windows, or legal compliance.

Security
- The app stores local messaging state using Android platform protections and encrypted local storage.
- Release builds are intended to use HTTPS transport and certificate pinning where configured.

Your choices
- You can reset local state on this phone from Privacy & account without deleting the remote account.
- You can delete the account from Privacy & account in the app.
- If you cannot access the app, use the public account-deletion help page linked from this screen.
