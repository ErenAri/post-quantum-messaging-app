# Account Deletion

Last updated: 2026-04-08

## Delete Your Account In The Android App

1. Open `Privacy & account`.
2. Review the current account shown on the screen.
3. Tap `Delete account`.
4. Confirm the deletion request.

This deletes the current account from the relay and wipes encrypted local state on the phone that submitted the request.

## If You Cannot Access The App

If you cannot sign into the app, submit an account-deletion request through the project issue tracker:

- https://github.com/ErenAri/post-quantum-messaging-app/issues

Include the exact `@username` that should be deleted and enough context for the pilot operator to identify the correct environment.

## Data That May Be Retained

Account deletion is intended to remove the account record and associated user data from the relay. Limited records may remain where necessary for security, abuse prevention, fraud handling, backup windows, audit integrity, or legal compliance.
